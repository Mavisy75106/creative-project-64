📊 About SimCity 3K in 4K

Inspired by the HN discussion about compressing 4K video to 3K resolution with minimal quality loss. This interactive demo simulates the compression pipeline: original data → resize → quality reduction → reconstruction. Adjust the compression slider and switch between views to see how different compression ratios affect the visual output. The block grid view reveals how pixels merge during downscaling, and the difference map highlights exactly where detail is lost.
